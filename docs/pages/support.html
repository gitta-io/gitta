What to include

  • The gitta version and your system.
  • The command you ran and its full output.
  • The output of gitta status --json, if the problem is about warnings or landing.
  • For Enterprise, the server's start lines and how you deployed it.

Do not send tokens, keys or your licence's signing material. Remove them from the output first.
